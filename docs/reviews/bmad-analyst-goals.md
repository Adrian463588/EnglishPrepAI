# BMAD Review — Product Analyst: Goals, Scope & Legal Provenance

- **Reviewer Role**: BMAD Product Analyst
- **Authoritative Spec**: `PRD.md`, `specs/001`..`004`

## Alignment Findings
1. **Exam Differentiation**: TOEFL ITP Level 1 (`ITP_L1`, 310–677), TOEFL iBT 2026 (`IBT_2026`, post-2026-01-21 1–6 scale), and IELTS Academic (`IELTS_ACADEMIC`, 0–9 bands) are structurally separated. Standard ITP Level 1 excludes Speaking/Writing from core mock exams.
2. **5 Packages per Exam**: 15 distinct themed tryout manifests (`ITP-L1-01..05`, `IBT26-01..05`, `IELTS-A-01..05`) ship with 12 original sample questions each (180 total) plus generated offline WAV audio. All 15 display `Sample · X/Y` counts transparently (`AC-04`) until full external licensed packs are imported.
3. **Offline & Privacy**: Zero mandatory network calls; no accounts, ads, telemetry, or cloud uploads. AI scores and coaching carry explicit non-official disclaimers.
4. **No Scope Creep**: Single `:app` module, zero Firebase/backend dependencies.
