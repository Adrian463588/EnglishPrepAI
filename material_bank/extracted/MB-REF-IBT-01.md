## TOEFL iBT 2026 Blueprint (Effective Jan 21, 2026) (p. 2-7)

Official ETS Jan 21, 2026 adaptive structure (~90 min): Reading 50 items (30 Academic Reading + 20 Daily Life & Complete the Words); Listening 47 items (25-29 Conversations & Choose a Response + 18-22 Lectures & Announcements); Writing 12 items (10 Build a Sentence + 1 Write an Email + 1 Write for an Academic Discussion); Speaking 11 items (7 Listen and Repeat + 4 Take an Interview).
