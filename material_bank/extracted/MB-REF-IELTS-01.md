## IELTS Academic Writing Band Descriptors (Task 1 & Task 2) (p. 1-9)

Public assessment criteria across 4 equal dimensions: Task Achievement (Task 1 requires a clear Overview of trends/comparisons without copying prompt phrases verbatim) / Task Response (Task 2 demands a consistent stance addressing all prompt parts); Coherence & Cohesion; Lexical Resource; Grammatical Range & Accuracy across Bands 1-9.
