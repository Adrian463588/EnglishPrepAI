## TOEFL ITP Level 1 Test Structure & Timing (p. 5-8)

Official ETS Level 1 specification: Section 1 Listening Comprehension (50 questions, ~35 minutes: Part A short dialogues, Part B extended conversations, Part C mini-lectures); Section 2 Structure & Written Expression (40 questions, 25 minutes: 1-15 Sentence Completion, 16-40 Error Identification); Section 3 Reading Comprehension (50 questions, 55 minutes across academic texts). Total: 140 questions in 115 minutes; scaled score range 310-677.
