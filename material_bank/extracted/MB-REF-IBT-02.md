## TOEFL iBT 2026 Score Reporting & CEFR Alignment (1.0-6.0) (p. 26-28)

Updated TOEFL iBT reports section and overall proficiency on a 1.0-6.0 half-band scale mapped directly to CEFR: Band 6.0 (C2) corresponds to legacy ~114-120; Band 5.0 (C1) ~ 95-106; Band 4.0 (B2) ~ 72-85; Band 3.0 (B1) ~ 44-57.
