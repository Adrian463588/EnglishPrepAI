## IELTS Speaking Public Band Descriptors (Parts 1, 2 & 3) (p. 1-4)

Evaluated equally across 4 dimensions: Fluency & Coherence (natural speech rate, cohesive linking, topic extension), Lexical Resource (accurate paraphrase and collocation), Grammatical Range & Accuracy, and Pronunciation (segmental clarity, rhythm, word/sentence stress, intonation).
